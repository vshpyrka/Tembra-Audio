# Tembra Audio privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Tembra Audio is a music app for tuning, playing to a metronome and recording on your phone or tablet. It is
developed by Victor Shpyrka. This privacy policy explains what the app does with information on your device. The
short version: the app keeps everything on your device and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account and no sign-in.
- It does not connect to the internet. The Android app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot
  send data anywhere.
- It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not use
  your device's advertising ID.
- We do not collect, sell or share any personal or sensitive information.

## What stays on your device

- **Microphone.** The app uses the microphone only when you ask it to: to hear your instrument in the tuner, and to
  record a take. The tuner analyses the sound as it arrives and does not save it. Recordings are saved as audio files
  in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Audio files you import.** When you pick a file in the system file picker, the app copies that file into its
  private storage. It cannot see any other files.
- **Projects and settings.** Your projects, recordings, imported audio and preferences are stored only in the app's
  private storage on your device.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the app shows one while the metronome or a project is playing, so
  you can stop playback from outside the app.

## Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can delete a project in the app,
delete all of the app's data by clearing its storage in your device settings, or remove everything by uninstalling
the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

## Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Because the app never
transmits your data, no data is sent over a network.

## Services provided by Google

- **Device backup.** If you have turned on backup on your Android device, Android may include the app's data in your
  device's backup to your Google account. That backup is provided by Google under Google's terms, and we have no
  access to it.
- **Google Play.** When you install the app from Google Play, Google processes information about the installation
  under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google may give us aggregated statistics and
  crash reports from users who have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports do not
  identify you to us.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling information differently, we will update this policy before that version is
released and change the effective date above. Earlier versions remain in this file's history.
